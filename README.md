# HR Analytics Dashboard
🚀 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

📊 I’m excited to share my latest Power BI dashboard focused on HR Analytics 
built to help organizations track and analyze employee attrition, demographics, and satisfaction trends effectively.

🎯 Key Objectives:

Monitor overall employee count, attrition rate, and active employees.

Understand attrition trends across departments, education fields, and age groups.

Analyze job satisfaction levels by role.

Visualize gender-wise attrition rates across different age brackets.

📈 Key Insights from the Dashboard:

Overall Employees: 1470 | Active Employees: 1233

Attrition Rate: 16.12% (237 Employees)

Average Age: 37 years

Highest Attrition Department: R&D (56.12%)

Top Age Group by Employees: 25–34 years

Education Field with Highest Attrition: Life Sciences (89)

Gender Trends: Higher attrition among females in multiple age groups.

Job Role with Highest Satisfaction (Rating 4): Research Scientist

💡 Business Impact:
This dashboard empowers HR teams to:
✅ Identify key drivers of attrition
✅ Improve employee retention strategies
✅ Optimize recruitment and engagement initiatives
